# OFA Driving School Website

The OFA website is an online platform designed for driving schools to streamline the management of courses, student registrations, and scheduling. It provides an intuitive interface for both instructors and learners, enabling easy access to educational resources, booking driving lessons, and tracking progress. The site emphasizes user-friendly navigation, secure data handling, and clear communication between staff and students.

## Main Features

- Online registration and profile management for students
- Scheduling and booking of driving lessons
- Access to learning materials and practice tests
- Progress tracking and feedback from instructors
- Secure payment processing for course fees
